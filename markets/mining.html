Sealing Solutions for Mining

Safety is the highest priority when it comes to working underground. This puts the greatest possible quality demands on mining machinery, products and service providers. At the same time, operating efficiency plays a key role in this industry as well. So it is essential to bring three factors into harmony: cost minimization and the simultaneous maximization of productivity and safety. The Sealing Solutions Group provides seals and sealing systems that live up to these standards.
